HOW THE JOURNEY WORKS

Build confidence
before production.

We want interns to understand the workflow before taking on suitable production-level responsibilities.

01

Practice projects

Start with practice work to explore tools, concepts, and development workflows in an appropriate learning environment.

02

Learn the process

Build familiarity, ask questions, and improve before moving on to responsibilities that match your readiness.

03

Contribute to projects

Depending on suitability and availability, contribute to NAVYON products and real-client projects with appropriate guidance.

04

Reflect & get certified

Complete the agreed internship requirements and receive certification according to NAVYON's completion criteria.
