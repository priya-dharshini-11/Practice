NAVYON Tech Solutions is a growing digital initiative focused on helping students and organizations bring ideas to life through practical web experiences and project support.

OUR APPROACH

Technology should
move ideas forward.

We believe a good digital project starts with understanding the person and purpose behind it. That means listening carefully, keeping the process collaborative, and making the result easy to use and explain.

So far, NAVYON Tech Solutions has helped students with portfolios and final-year projects, and is working on a foundation website. We also create opportunities for creativity and participation, including the Yuga — 2K26 logo redesign competition.

WHY WE STARTED NAVYON

We wanted to create the opportunity
we wished we had found ourselves.

Our journey began with the difficulty of finding internships that offered genuine, hands-on programming experience. We saw how an internship could become a requirement to complete rather than an opportunity to learn, especially when students had little chance to participate in meaningful work.

At the same time, we wanted to build a future using skills we already had, solve real problems, and work towards supporting our families. NAVYON Enterprises grew from that ambition: create practical opportunities for students while building something useful and sustainable together.

Our approach brings together practice projects, suitable contributions to NAVYON products and real-client work, and certification based on internship completion criteria.
